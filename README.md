# Angus Forrest

I'm Angus, a mathematician who appreciates physical systems,

information theory, and science communication.

I specialise in constructing data driven models from large datasets,

building complicated numerical simulations, and writing code for high

performance computing. I practice the dark arts with CUDA, Rust,

SwiftUI, and React/TS; from number crunching to visualisation. What gets

me out of bed in the morning is assembling systems that take into

account real world limitations, solving other people's problems, and

applying new research to old problems.


While my strength lies in novel technologies, I have put in the hard

yards communicating effectively to a wide variety of backgrounds from my

time teaching at my alma mater's mathematics department. I love the

challenge of acting as a bridge between technically competent thinkers

and business focused individuals (not to say there isn't overlap!). I

have first-hand experience of the usefulness of multiple sets of eyes on

the same problem illuminating it from different angles.


Beyond modelling and communication, I also focus on maintaining

continuity. I appreciate working in collaboration with domain experts to

understand the transition between the new and old.


If you want to solve people's problems here and now, please reach out!
